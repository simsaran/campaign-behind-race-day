# The Campaign Behind Race Day

I was looking at how premium credit card programs show up around major events like the Canadian Grand Prix: early ticket access, exclusive offers, hospitality packages, loyalty point redemptions, and in-app event messaging.

It made me think about the work behind those campaigns. Someone has to choose the right cardholder segment, plan the offer, coordinate creative, legal, compliance, translation, and digital teams, then measure whether the campaign actually worked.

So I built a full customer marketing campaign package around that idea.

---

## What this project is

**The Campaign Behind Race Day** is an end-to-end customer marketing project for a fictional premium Canadian loyalty program sponsoring the Canadian Grand Prix.

It includes campaign planning, cardholder segmentation, stakeholder coordination, compliance checkpoints, KPI tracking, and post-campaign reporting.

The goal was to build something that feels like real internal marketing work in financial services, not just a creative campaign idea.

## What I built

- A one-page campaign brief with the objective, target segment, offer, channel plan, timeline, and KPI targets
- A Python-based cardholder segment analysis

- A Streamlit dashboard to track campaign performance by segment and channel
- A post-campaign report with results, insights, compliance notes, and next-step recommendations

---

## Key segments analyzed

| Segment | Purpose |
|---|---|
| High-Spend Travel Cardmembers | Premium audience most likely to respond to access and hospitality offers |
| Active Rewards Redeemers | Cardmembers already engaged with loyalty points and experience-based redemptions |
| 90-Day Lapsed Cardmembers | Re-engagement audience with lower activity but potential lifecycle value |

---

## What the results showed

Active rewards redeemers had the strongest engagement because the offer matched an existing behaviour: using points for experiences.

High-spend travel cardmembers generated the highest revenue per cardholder because the campaign matched their premium travel behaviour.

Lapsed cardmembers had lower conversion, but the campaign still created a useful re-engagement opportunity through timely event-based messaging.

The dashboard tracks open rate, click rate, conversion rate, redemption lift, revenue per cardholder, customer feedback score, and compliance checkpoint pass rate.
